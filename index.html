About Xu Chen

Born in Chengdu and now based between Shanghai and Tokyo, I've spent the last decade documenting the world through a lens. My work spans fine art, editorial commissions, and commercial projects for clients including National Geographic, Vogue, and The New York Times.

I believe photography is not about capturing what something looks like — but what it feels like. Every frame is a conversation between light, subject, and moment.
